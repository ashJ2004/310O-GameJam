## Report:

For the theme, “Everything is Alive” I decided to make a game oriented on the player being able to possess household objects to interact in the world and progress further through the level. To best accommodate this theme, I decided for the level to take place in a kitchen, due to the amount of household appliances that could be found there, allowing for a variety of different tools to possess and different areas to explore. 
To play the third person game, use WASD to move, and mouse to move your camera. The E key is used to interact with most of the objects in the level. Shift can be used to sprint as the player and the sponge, and space is used to jump(if allowed or not used for another function). Due to the nature of the different objects, their keybinds are different, which is why in my development I include a legend when the player enters a given object that tells the user what keys are mapped to what. The only thing that remains the same between the objects is WASD to move, and the mouse to look with the camera. Everything else that the object is allowed to do is located within this legend, and will be updated accordingly throughout the game. Z is used to exit the objects you have possessed, and X can be used to reset the player at the most recent checkpoint, given that they aren’t possessing an object.
